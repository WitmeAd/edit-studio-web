Working with a client usually means juggling WhatsApp photos, folders, spreadsheets and a PDF put together by hand. édit Pro brings it all into one studio, so you spend your time on styling, not on sorting files.

## What does édit Pro do?

- **My studio.** All your clients at a glance, with what you have pending with each one.
- **Each client’s profile.** Her color analysis, her style archetype, her documents and her photos, together.
- **Her digital closet.** Upload photos of her clothes, several items per photo and even from WhatsApp, or paste a store link. The items are cut out and sorted while you keep working; all you do is check them.
- **Looks built around one piece.** Choose the main piece and the look builder shows you what’s missing to complete the look from her closet.
- **Try it on her.** See your client wearing the look, on her photo, before you send it.
- **Plans and dossier.** Organize a trip, a week or an event with her looks and download a PDF styling dossier with your brand, to send her by WhatsApp or email.
- **Chat and proposals.** Your looks, plans and recommendations reach her app, and she gives you her feedback on each one.

## How do clients find you?

In two ways:

1. **You invite them.** Enter her name and email and she gets the invitation. Her app account is included when she works with you.
2. **They find you in édit.** App users browse consultants, see your portfolio and send you a request. Requests arrive in My studio, under “New requests”, and you decide whether to take them on as clients.

Your public profile shows your introduction, your portfolio and the inspirations you choose to share. You can stop receiving requests when your schedule is full.

## Who is it for?

For image consultants, stylists and personal shoppers who work with individual clients, in person or remotely. If you do color analysis, you can import your color analysis report (PDF, image or Word) and the client’s profile fills itself in, ready for you to review.

## Which devices does it work on?

On the web, from your computer, and in the app for iPhone and Android, with the same account. The app is available in English, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese, so you can work with clients in other countries.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I sign up as an image consultant?

On the édit website, with “Create my consultant account”. You start with an édit Pro trial, and your profile shows how many days you have left.

### What happens to my clients when the trial ends?

Your clients, their looks and their items stay where they are. What stops is trying on looks, creating their images and analyzing new items, until you activate your professional plan.

### Do my clients have to pay anything?

Not to work with you: the account of a client you invite includes her digital closet, your looks and plans, the chat and her dossier. If she also wants to use the app’s Premium features on her own, she can subscribe herself.

### Can I put my brand on the dossiers?

Yes. The PDF styling dossier carries your brand, and you send it yourself, by WhatsApp or email.
