A virtual try-on for clothes lets you see an outfit on you before you put it on. In édit it isn’t a mannequin or an avatar: it’s your photo, with your clothes.

## How does édit’s virtual try-on work?

1. **Your digital closet.** The pieces come from your closet in édit. If you don’t have one yet, start by [digitizing your closet](/armario-digital/).
2. **A look.** Choose one of the looks édit suggests or put together your own.
3. **Your photo.** Upload a full-length photo of yourself, facing the camera, in good light.
4. **Try on me.** édit shows you the look on your photo.

The image is a preview: it’s there to show proportions, colors and the overall feel of the outfit, not to check sizes.

## Why try clothes on with your phone?

- **Decide without trying on five things.** See two or three options and keep the one you like.
- **Mix more freely.** Try combinations you’d never even consider in front of the mirror.
- **Plan ahead.** For a trip, a wedding or a week of meetings, see the looks before you pack.
- **Shop smarter.** Before you buy something, think about what you already own that you’d wear it with.

## Which photo works best?

A full-length photo, facing the camera, in natural light, wearing clothes that don’t hide your silhouette. A plain background helps. If you don’t like the result, try another photo: the framing makes a big difference.

## Can my image consultant use it?

Yes. If you work with an image consultant in édit, she can try her looks on your photos and send them to your app. More in [online image consultant](/asesora-de-imagen-online/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the virtual try-on free?

The virtual try-on is part of the édit Premium subscription. Downloading the app is free, and when you sign up you get a few days’ trial without entering any payment details.

### Can I try on clothes from a store?

The try-on works with the items in your closet in édit. If a piece is in your closet, you can combine it and try it on like any other.

### Who sees my photos?

Your try-on photos are saved in your profile, and you can add or delete them whenever you like. If you work with an image consultant, she sees them too so she can try her looks on you. How we handle your data is explained in the [privacy policy](https://dashboard.editstudio.app/legal/privacidad).
