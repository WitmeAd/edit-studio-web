Color analysis

Your color analysis, in every look

Updated on November 15, 2026

In short. In édit, your color analysis is part of your style profile. If you’ve already had your colors done, you choose your season from the 12, and the looks the app suggests take it into account. If you don’t know it yet, you can choose an image consultant in édit to do your analysis and save it to your profile.

Color analysis looks at which colors flatter you based on your skin, your eyes and your hair. Knowing your season doesn’t help much if it stays on a color swatch card in a drawer. In édit it lives inside your closet: in your style profile and in the looks it suggests.

How does color analysis work in édit?

  • If you already know your season, you choose it in your style profile from the 12 seasons.
  • If you don’t, you tap “I don’t know it yet” and keep using the app as usual. Whenever you like, an image consultant can do your analysis.
  • Your looks take it into account. The looks édit suggests start from your clothes and your season.

What are the 12 color seasons?

It’s the most widely used color analysis system. It starts from four seasons (spring, summer, autumn and winter) and splits each one into three, depending on which trait dominates: the temperature of the color, its intensity or its depth.

Who does my color analysis?

An image consultant. In édit you can browse professional consultants, see their looks and choose the one who’s right for you. She does the analysis and saves it to your profile, and from then on her suggestions and the app’s come in your colors. More in online image consultant.

What if my closet isn’t in my colors?

You don’t need to throw anything away. Color matters most near your face: a shirt, a sweater, a scarf. Away from your face (pants, skirts, shoes) there’s much more room to play. Start by pairing what you have with one piece from your palette on top, and use your season as a guide for what you buy next.

Frequently asked questions

Does édit tell me my color season?

Not from a photo of you. If you already know it, you choose it in your profile. If not, an image consultant does your analysis and saves it to your profile.

How many color seasons are there in édit?

Twelve: the four classic seasons, each divided into three subtypes.

Is there a style quiz as well as color analysis?

Yes. An eight-question quiz reveals your style archetype. Together with your color analysis, it guides what édit suggests.
