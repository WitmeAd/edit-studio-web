How you work with each client.

From profile to dossier

  1. 01 · Her profile

    Her color analysis and her style, at hand.

    Import your color analysis report as a PDF, image or Word file and her profile fills itself in, ready for you to review. Her archetype, her documents and her photos, in the same place.

  2. 02 · Her closet

    Her closet, digitized without the effort.

    Upload photos of her clothes, several items per photo and even from WhatsApp, or paste a store link. The items are cut out and sorted while you keep working.

  3. 03 · Her looks

    Looks you see on her before you send them.

    Choose the main piece and the look builder shows you what’s missing to complete the look from her closet. Then you see it on her photo, before you send it.

    Plans and dossier, with your brand.

    Organize a trip, a week or an event with her looks and download a PDF styling dossier with your brand, ready to send her by WhatsApp or email.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign up as an image consultant?

On the édit website, with “Create my consultant account”. You start with an édit Pro trial, and your profile shows how many days you have left.

What happens to my clients when the trial ends?

Your clients, their looks and their items stay where they are. What stops is trying on looks, creating their images and analyzing new items, until you activate your professional plan.

Do my clients have to pay anything?

Not to work with you: the account of a client you invite includes her digital closet, your looks and plans, the chat and her dossier. If she also wants to use the app’s Premium features on her own, she can subscribe herself.

Can I put my brand on the dossiers?

Yes. The PDF styling dossier carries your brand, and you send it yourself, by WhatsApp or email.
