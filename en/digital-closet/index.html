Digital closet app

Your digital closet, on your phone and in order

Updated on November 15, 2026

In short. With édit, all your clothes are on your phone. Upload a full-length photo of you and the app picks out the clothes you’re wearing, or photograph your clothes one item at a time. Each item is cut out, gets its own details and is sorted by type, color and season. From there you create looks, plan trips and see what you’re missing before you shop.

A digital closet is your wardrobe on a screen: every item with its photo and its details, organized and at hand. It lets you see what you own at a glance, put outfits together without taking anything off the hanger, and stop forgetting the pieces at the back of the drawer.

How do you digitize your closet with édit?

There are two ways, and you can use both.

  1. A full-length photo of you. Upload a photo of yourself wearing an outfit and édit picks out the clothes you have on. Each one is saved as a new item in your closet. With a few photos from different days, you’ll have a good share of what you wear most.
  2. One item at a time. Take a photo of a piece of clothing, on the bed or on a hanger, and édit recognizes it.

Either way, the app removes the background from each item and fills in its details: type, color and season. You check them before saving.

What do I do with my digital closet?

Use it to get dressed. That’s where you’ll notice the difference:

  • Looks with your own clothes. édit suggests combinations with your own pieces, and you can put together your own, save them and wear them again.
  • Virtual try-on. See a look on a photo of you before you put it on. More on that in the virtual try-on.
  • Your week. Every week, a look for each day, planned around your schedule, the weather and your style.
  • Trips and events. With plans, you choose the looks for each day and check off what’s already in the suitcase.
  • Smarter shopping. Before you buy, see what you’re really missing and what you already have twice.

Do I have to upload all my clothes at once?

No. Start with the ten or fifteen pieces you wear most and add the rest little by little. That first batch is enough to start putting outfits together.

Why does a digital closet help you dress better?

Because almost nobody wears everything they own. We reach for what we can see and what we already know works. When your clothes are organized on a screen, you rediscover forgotten pieces, spot new combinations and buy less on impulse.

Your closet doesn’t need to be full. It needs to make sense.

Frequently asked questions

Is the digital closet app free?

Downloading édit is free. Some features, such as the looks édit suggests, the virtual try-on and the weekly selection, are part of the Premium subscription. When you sign up you get a few days’ trial without entering any payment details, and you see the price in the app before you confirm.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. édit is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. The app is available in English, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese.

Can I get several items from a single photo?

Yes. If you upload a full-length photo of yourself, édit detects the clothes you’re wearing and creates a page for each one.

What if an item’s details aren’t right?

You check them and correct them before saving: type, color and season. The better the details, the easier it is to find the item and the better your combinations will be.
