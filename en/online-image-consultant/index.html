Online image consultant

Online image consultant, working with your real closet

Updated on November 15, 2026

In short. In édit you can work with an image consultant without meeting in person. You browse professional consultants, see their looks and send a request to the one who’s right for you. Once she accepts, she sees your digital closet, knows your color analysis and your style, and sends looks, plans and suggestions to your app.

An image consultant helps you dress for who you are: your body, your colors and your everyday life. Online, the problem is usually that she can’t see your clothes. In édit she can: she works on your digital closet.

How does online image consulting work in édit?

  1. You choose. In the Consultant tab, you browse professional image consultants, read their introductions and look at their portfolios and looks.
  2. You send a request. Tell her what you’re looking for: a style refresh, a trip, an event, getting more out of your closet.
  3. She sees your real closet. Once she accepts, she works with the clothes you already have in édit, along with your color analysis and your style archetype.
  4. Straight to your app. Her looks, her plans for a trip or a season and her suggestions arrive inside édit. You can also chat.

You can disconnect from your consultant whenever you like.

What are the advantages over in-person consulting?

  • She works with what you already own. She doesn’t start from scratch or send you off to buy a new wardrobe.
  • Everything stays saved. The looks she suggests are in your app, ready to wear again whenever you like.
  • No matter where you live. You choose the professional who’s right for you, not the one who happens to be nearby.
  • You can see yourself in her looks. With the virtual try-on, she can try her suggestions on your photos.

What can an image consultant do for me?

It depends on each professional, but it usually includes:

  • a color analysis to find your season (more in color analysis);
  • understanding your style and how you want to look;
  • sorting out your closet: what works, what’s missing and what you don’t need;
  • looks for everyday life, for work or for a special occasion;
  • a plan for a trip, an event or a season, with what you need to take.

Not sure who to choose? Look at each consultant’s portfolio and looks before you send a request.

Are you an image consultant?

édit is also your digital studio: your clients, their closets and your looks, all in one place. You’ll find it in édit professionals.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see a consultant’s work before choosing her?

Yes. Her profile shows her introduction, her portfolio and the inspirations she shares. If you like one, “Recreate it with your wardrobe” shows you which pieces you already own and which looks you can create.

How do we agree on the service?

In your request, you tell her what you’re looking for. Once she accepts, you agree on the terms of her service directly with her.

Can I change consultants?

Yes. You can disconnect whenever you like and choose another professional.
